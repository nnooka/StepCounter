How to export from MI Fitness
  1. Open the MI Fitness app on your phone.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom-right icon).
  3. Tap Settings → Export data.
  4. Select the date range and tap Export.
  5. Share the resulting .zip file to your computer and upload it here.
